# Workforce management

**Source Citation**: [Workforce management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workforce_management)
**Domain**: Career

---

Workforce management (WFM) is an institutional process that aims to optimize productivity, competency, and engagement of an organization's workforce. Generally this process relies on software to plan business needs more effectively than through a spreadsheet. WFM typically includes all activities needed to maintain a productive workforce: human resource management, performance and training management, budgeting, forecasting, and scheduling.
