# Intermittent fasting

**Source Citation**: [Wikipedia Open Corpus (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermittent_fasting)
**Domain**: Wellbeing

---

Intermittent fasting (IF) is any of various meal timing schedules that cycle between voluntary fasting and non-fasting over a given period. Methods of intermittent fasting include alternate-day fasting, periodic fasting, such as the 5:2 diet, and daily time-restricted eating.
