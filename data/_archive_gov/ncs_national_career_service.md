Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using NCS.

What is NCS?

The National Career Service (NCS) is a one-stop solution offering a wide range of employment and career-related services to the citizens of India, including job search, job matching, career content, counselling, and job fair information. It is an initiative of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India.

Who can register on the portal?

Job Seekers, Employers, Skill Providers, Career Counsellors, Local Service Providers, Career Centers, Placement Organisations, Households, and Government Departments.

Is NCS free to use?

Yes. NCS does not charge any fee at any stage of registration, job application, or interview processing. Jobseekers should not pay fees to employers.

Is a mobile app available?

Yes. The NCS mobile app is available for finding and applying for jobs. It can be downloaded from the app store links on the portal home page.

How can I avoid fake NCS communication?

Some websites and individuals falsely claim an association with NCS or use its logo. Please verify information only on the official website,

www.ncs.gov.in

. You may contact NCS at support.ncs@gov.in or on the toll-free helpline 1514. Cyber fraud can be reported on 1930.

Beware of Misleading Communication

It has been reported to us that some websites/employers/persons claim to have association with National Career Service (NCS)/Ministry of Labour & Employment (MoLE) are using the logo of NCS. They are fraudulent and tarnishing the image and reputation of NCS.

NCS services are free of cost. NCS does not charge any fee at any stage of registration, job application, interview processing and other employment related services.

Jobseekers should not pay fees to employers.
