# Decision Framework: Macronutrient Distribution & Caloric Energy Balance

## 📌 Executive Summary
Calculating daily energy requirements (TDEE) and setting protein, carbohydrate, and fat ratios.

## 📊 Caloric Balance Formula
- **Maintenance Calories (TDEE)**: BMR × Activity Factor (1.2 to 1.5).
- **Fat Loss Surplus/Deficit**: Moderate 300–500 kcal daily deficit (~0.5 kg loss per week).
- **Macro Split**: 30% Protein, 40% Complex Carbohydrates (whole grains, vegetables), 30% Healthy Fats (nuts, seeds, olive oil).
