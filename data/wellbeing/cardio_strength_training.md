# Decision Framework: Exercise Programming — Resistance vs Cardiovascular Training

## 📌 Executive Summary
Structuring weekly exercise routines combining resistance training (strength/hypertrophy) and Zone 2 cardio for metabolic longevity.

## 🏋️ Ideal Weekly Split
- **Resistance Training (3–4 days/week)**: Progressive overload compounding movements (Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, Rows) for muscle retention and bone density.
- **Zone 2 Cardiovascular Training (150 mins/week)**: Low-intensity steady-state cardio (brisk walking, cycling at conversational pace) for mitochondrial health and fat oxidation.
