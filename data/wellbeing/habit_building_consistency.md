# Decision Framework: Behavioral Habit Stacking & Consistency Building

## 📌 Executive Summary
Building sustainable daily habits and overcoming motivation fluctuations using behavioral psychology principles.

## 🔁 The Habit Loop Protocol
1. **Cue / Anchor**: Attach new desired habit to an existing automatic daily habit (e.g., "After I pour my morning coffee, I will write my top 3 tasks").
2. **Two-Minute Rule**: Scale down new habits so they take under 2 minutes to start (e.g. read 1 page, do 5 pushups).
3. **Never Miss Twice**: If you break a habit 1 day, prioritize completing it the next day to protect identity momentum.
