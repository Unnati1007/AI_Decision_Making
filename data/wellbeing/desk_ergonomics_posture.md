# Decision Framework: Desk Ergonomics & Postural Health for Tech Workers

## 📌 Executive Summary
Preventing repetitive strain injury (RSI), neck strain, and lower back pain during long computer work hours.

## 📐 Ergonomic Setup Rules
- **Monitor Height**: Top third of monitor screen at eye level.
- **Elbow & Wrist Angle**: 90-degree arm bend with wrists neutral (not bent upward).
- **20-20-20 Rule**: Every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent visual fatigue.
