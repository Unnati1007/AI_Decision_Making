# Decision Framework: Occupational Burnout & Stress Recovery

## 📌 Executive Summary
Identifying workplace burnout stages and implementing physiological and behavioral reset protocols.

## 🩺 Burnout Identification vs General Fatigue
- **Burnout Symptoms**: Chronic cynicism/detachment, emotional exhaustion, diminished sense of efficacy despite long working hours.
- **Physiological Reset Protocol**: Daily 10-minute non-sleep deep rest (NSDR), physical exercise to complete stress response cycles, strict end-of-workday cutoff times.
