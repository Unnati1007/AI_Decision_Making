# Decision Framework: Anxiety Management & Evidence-Based Cognitive Techniques

## 📌 Executive Summary
Managing acute performance anxiety and chronic stress using Cognitive Behavioral (CBT) grounding frameworks.

## 🧠 Grounding Frameworks
- **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding**: Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste to halt anxiety spiral.
- **Box Breathing (4x4x4x4)**: Inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s to activate parasympathetic nervous system.
- **Professional Therapy Threshold**: Seek licensed therapy if anxiety impairs daily sleep, work deliverables, or personal relationships for > 2 consecutive weeks.
