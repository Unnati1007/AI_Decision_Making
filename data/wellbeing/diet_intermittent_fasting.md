# Decision Framework: Intermittent Fasting & Protein-First Nutrition

## 📌 Executive Summary
Implementing 16:8 time-restricted feeding and protein-prioritized meal planning for sustainable body composition and energy regulation.

## 🥗 16:8 Fasting Protocol
- **Eating Window**: 8 hours (e.g. 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM).
- **Fasting Window**: 16 hours (water, black coffee, herbal tea allowed).
- **Protein Target**: 1.6g to 2.2g per kg of target body weight to preserve lean muscle mass during weight loss.
