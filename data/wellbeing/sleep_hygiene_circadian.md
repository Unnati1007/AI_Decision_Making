# Decision Framework: Sleep Hygiene & Circadian Alignment

## 📌 Executive Summary
Optimizing 7–9 hour sleep quality through circadian light exposure and sleep routine protocols.

## 🌙 Sleep Optimization Protocol
1. **Morning Light**: Get 10–15 minutes of direct sunlight within 60 minutes of waking to anchor cortisol awakening response.
2. **Caffeine Cutoff**: Avoid caffeine consumption 8–10 hours before target sleep time.
3. **Blue Light Elimination**: Turn off bright screens 90 minutes before bed or use blue-blocking glasses to maximize natural melatonin release.
