# Decision Framework: Remote Work Boundaries & Digital Detox

## 📌 Executive Summary
Preventing work-life boundary dissolution in high-pressure remote and hybrid knowledge work roles.

## 🛑 Boundary Enforcement Protocols
- **Physical Shutdown**: Use a dedicated work workstation; close work laptop and put out of sight at end of workday.
- **Asynchronous Communication**: Set clear notification cutoff hours on Slack/Teams (e.g. 7:00 PM).
- **Weekend Disconnect**: Zero work email checks during weekends to allow full psychological recovery.
