# Decision Framework: Psychological Resilience & Handling Setbacks

## 📌 Executive Summary
Building emotional regulation strategies during high-stakes work feedback, project failures, or career setbacks.

## 🛡️ Resilience Frameworks
- **Stoic Control Dichotomy**: Separate events into things within your control (effort, reaction, skills) vs outside your control (market economic conditions, manager opinions).
- **Reframing Imposter Syndrome**: Recognize feeling unequipped is a standard cognitive byproduct of operating at the edge of your growth zone.
