# Decision Framework: Tenant & Landlord Rights (Rent Agreements in India)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Navigating rental agreements, security deposit refunds, and tenancy disputes.

## 📋 Legal Tenancy Rules
1. **11-Month Rental Agreement**: Standard practice in India to bypass registration requirements under the Registration Act.
2. **Security Deposit Refund**: Landlord must return deposit within 30 days of vacation, deducting only documented damage costs (excluding normal wear and tear).
3. **Eviction Notice**: Minimum 1-month written notice required from either party as per standard agreement terms.
