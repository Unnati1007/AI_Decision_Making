# Decision Framework: Contract Review & Legal Agreement Checklist

## 📌 Executive Summary
A structured legal evaluation guide before signing binding employment, freelance, or commercial agreements.

## 📋 Critical Clause Checklist
1. **Termination & Notice Period**: Ensure clear mutual termination rights (e.g., 30-day written notice).
2. **Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment**: Confirm whether IP produced outside working hours or using personal equipment is protected.
3. **Limitation of Liability**: Ensure liability is capped at contract value rather than unlimited exposure.
4. **Governing Law & Jurisdiction**: Verify jurisdiction is local or reasonable to prevent costly out-of-station litigation.
