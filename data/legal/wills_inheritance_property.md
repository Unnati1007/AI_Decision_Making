# Decision Framework: Estate Planning & Property Succession

## 📌 Executive Summary
Drafting legally valid wills and understanding property succession laws in India.

## 📋 Will Registration & Succession
1. **Valid Will Requirements**: Written document signed by testator in presence of **two independent witnesses**.
2. **Registration**: Optional but strongly recommended to prevent authenticity challenges.
3. **Intestate Succession**: If no will exists, property is distributed according to personal succession laws (Hindu Succession Act, Indian Succession Act).
