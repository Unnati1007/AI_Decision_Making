# Decision Framework: Freelancer & Vendor Payment Protection Agreements

## 📌 Executive Summary
Protecting independent contractors from non-payment and scope creep.

## 📋 Freelance Agreement Safeguards
1. **Milestone Payments**: 30% advance deposit + milestone-based deliverables (never 100% post-delivery).
2. **Late Payment Penalty**: Specify 1.5% to 2% monthly interest penalty on invoices overdue beyond 30 days.
3. **Scope Creep Clause**: Limit revisions (e.g. max 2 revision rounds) with fixed hourly rate for additional changes.
