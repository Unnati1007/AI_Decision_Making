# Decision Framework: Consumer Protection Rights & Filing Grievances

## 📌 Executive Summary
Exercising rights under Consumer Protection Act 2019 for defective products or deficient services.

## 📋 Consumer Action Steps
1. **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)**: Lodge online complaint at consumerhelpline.gov.in before court filing.
2. **Legal Notice**: Send formal legal demand notice allowing 15 days for resolution.
3. **Consumer Forum Filing**: File case at District Consumer Commission for claims up to ₹50 Lakhs (e-daakhil portal).
