# Decision Framework: Intellectual Property Protection (India)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Protecting business brands, creative works, and tech inventions through Trademarks, Copyrights, and Patents.

## ⚖️ IP Category Comparison
- **Trademark (TM / ®)**: Protects brand names, logos, slogans. Valid for 10 years (renewable). First-to-file principle applies in India.
- **Copyright (©)**: Protects original literary, artistic, and software source code. Automatic upon creation, but registration provides evidentiary proof in court.
- **Patent**: Protects novel technical inventions for 20 years. Examination process takes 2 to 4 years.
