# Decision Framework: Labor Laws & Employee Legal Rights

## 📌 Executive Summary
Understanding statutory employee rights including maternity benefits, POSH workplace harassment laws, and severance.

## ⚖️ Statutory Employee Rights
- **POSH Act**: Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) mandatory for organizations with 10+ employees.
- **Maternity Benefit Act**: 26 weeks fully paid maternity leave for female employees.
- **Gratuity Act**: Mandatory payment after 5 continuous years of service (15 days salary per completed year).
