# Decision Framework: Startup Formation & Co-Founder Equity Vesting

## 📌 Executive Summary
Legal steps for registering a startup (Pvt Ltd vs LLP) and structuring founder equity agreements.

## 📋 Startup Legal Priorities
1. **Entity Selection**: Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) is mandatory for venture capital fundraising. LLP is suitable for bootstrapped agencies.
2. **Co-Founder Agreement**: Define equity splits, reverse vesting (standard 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff), and founder departure protocols.
3. **IP Assignment**: Ensure founders assign all code and designs created to the company entity.
