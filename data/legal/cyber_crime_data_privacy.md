# Decision Framework: Cyber Crime Reporting & Data Protection

## 📌 Executive Summary
Handling online financial fraud, identity theft, data breaches, and IT Act reporting.

## 🛡️ Immediate Incident Response
1. **Financial Fraud Freeze**: Call **1930 Cyber Helpline** within the "Golden Hour" (first 2 hours) to freeze stolen funds in bank pipelines.
2. **National Cyber Crime Portal**: File official complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with transaction receipts and screenshot proof.
3. **Data Privacy Compliance**: DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act requires user consent for collecting personal data.
