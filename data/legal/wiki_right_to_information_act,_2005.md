# Right to Information Act, 2005

**Source Citation**: [Wikipedia Open Corpus (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_Information_Act%2C_2005)
**Domain**: Legal

---

The Right to Information Act, 2005 is an act of the Parliament of India and the Delhi Legislative Assembly which sets out the rules and procedures regarding citizens' right to access information. It replaced the former Freedom of Information Act, 2002.
