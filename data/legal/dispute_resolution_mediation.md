# Decision Framework: Commercial Dispute Resolution — Mediation vs Litigation

## 📌 Executive Summary
Resolving business and contractual disputes through alternative dispute resolution (ADR) vs Indian court litigation.

## ⚖️ Resolution Path Comparison
- **Mediation / Arbitration**: Fast (3 to 6 months), confidential, lower fees, mutually agreed arbitrator. Highly recommended for commercial agreements.
- **Court Litigation**: Public record, formal evidence requirements, long timelines (5 to 15+ years in Indian civil courts).
