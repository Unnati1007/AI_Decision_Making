# Freelancer

**Source Citation**: [Wikipedia Open Corpus (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freelancer)
**Domain**: Career

---

Freelance, freelancer, and freelance worker are common terms for a person who is self-employed and not necessarily committed to a particular employer long-term. Freelance workers are sometimes represented by a company or a temporary agency that resells freelance labor to clients. Others work independently or use professional associations or websites to get work.
