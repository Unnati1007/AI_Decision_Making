# Decision Framework: MBA vs Job Continuation

## 📌 Executive Summary
Deciding whether to pursue an MBA or continue working is a critical career milestone. The decision hinges on work experience, current compensation, long-term goals, and financial ROI.

## 📊 Experience Matrix
- **0–2 Years Experience**: Focus on core technical skill building. A 2-year full-time MBA (via CAT/GMAT) is ideal for freshers aiming to enter consulting or investment banking.
- **2–5 Years Experience**: The sweet spot for top tier 2-year MBA programs (IIM A/B/C, ISB, INSEAD, Wharton). High career switch potential.
- **5+ Years Experience**: Avoid 2-year MBA programs due to opportunity cost. Opt for 1-Year Executive MBA (EPGP/PGPX) or specialized executive education.

## 💡 Financial ROI Formula
- `Opportunity Cost` = (Current Annual Salary × Program Duration) + Tuition Fees + Living Expenses
- `Target Post-MBA Salary` = Should be at least 1.6x to 2x of total program annual cost to achieve break-even within 3.5 years.

## ⚖️ Key Pros & Cons
### Pursuing MBA:
- **Pros**: 50–80% average salary hike in top institutes, fast-track to leadership/management roles, strong professional network.
- **Cons**: High tuition fees (₹15L–₹35L in India, $100k+ abroad), loss of 1–2 years salary.

### Continuing Job:
- **Pros**: Zero career gap, continuous income and compounding, practical hands-on experience.
- **Cons**: Potential ceiling on management promotions in traditional corporate structures.

## 🚀 Actionable Recommendation
If you have 2–5 years experience and earn below ₹15 LPA, a top-tier MBA delivers positive ROI. If you already earn > ₹25 LPA, prioritize internal mobility or executive MBA.
