# Decision Framework: Managing Layoffs & Explaining Career Gaps

## 📌 Executive Summary
Navigating unexpected job loss or explaining intentional career breaks requires a proactive, strategic communication framework.

## 🛠️ Layoff Recovery Steps
1. **Immediate Financial Audit**: Secure severance package details, insurance continuity, and calculate emergency runway (aim for 6 months).
2. **Network Activation**: Reach out directly to former colleagues, alumni, and recruiters with a concise, non-emotional status update.
3. **Upskilling Narrative**: Frame the time between jobs as an active period of learning, consulting, or project execution.

## 💬 Framing Career Breaks in Interviews
- **Transparency & Positivity**: State the reason clearly (layoff, family care, health, upskilling) without placing blame on former employers.
- **Project Tangibility**: Demonstrate proof of skill retention via open-source contributions, freelancing, or certifications completed during the gap.
