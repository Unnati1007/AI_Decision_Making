# Curriculum vitae

**Source Citation**: [Wikipedia Open Corpus (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curriculum_vitae)
**Domain**: Career

---

In English, a curriculum vitae, commonly known as CV, is a short written summary of a person's career, qualifications, and education. This is the most common usage in British English. In North America, the term résumé is used, referring to a short career summary, whereas CVs are much longer than resumes.
