# Decision Framework: Tech Leveling & Total Compensation (TC) Bands

## 📌 Executive Summary
Understanding technology career levels and Total Compensation (TC) breakdown is critical for evaluating offers, setting 3-year promotion goals, and negotiating fair market compensation.

## 📊 Software Engineering Leveling Matrix
| Industry Level | Title (Big Tech / Unicorns) | Years of Experience (YoE) | Key Scope & Expectations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L3 / Junior** | SDE-1 / Associate Engineer | 0–2 Years | Task-level execution under senior guidance; focus on code quality & bug fixes. |
| **L4 / Mid-Level** | SDE-2 / Software Engineer | 2–5 Years | Module-level ownership; independently designs & delivers features end-to-end. |
| **L5 / Senior** | SDE-3 / Senior Software Engineer | 5–8 Years | System-level architecture; drives team technical direction & mentors juniors. |
| **L6 / Staff** | Staff Engineer / Tech Lead Manager | 8–12 Years | Multi-team impact; solves complex cross-system architectural bottlenecks. |
| **L7 / Principal** | Principal Engineer / Architect | 12+ Years | Company-wide technical strategy, multi-year roadmap & infrastructure scale. |

## 💰 Total Compensation (TC) Component Breakdown
- **Base Salary**: Fixed monthly payout. Primary anchor for loan eligibility, EPF contributions, and gratuity calculations.
- **Performance Bonus / Variable**: Annual or semi-annual payout linked to company performance (0–20% of base).
- **Equity (RSUs / ESOPs)**:
  - **RSUs (Public Big Tech)**: Liquid stock units. Standard 4-year vesting schedule (25% per year) with no cliff on refreshers.
  - **ESOPs (Private Startups)**: Options to buy shares. Standard 4-year vesting with a **1-year cliff** (0% vested before 12 months).
- **Joining / Sign-on Bonus**: One-time cash bonus spread across Year 1 and Year 2 to compensate for forfeited stock/bonuses.

## 🗺️ Location & Cost of Living (CoL) Multipliers (India Context)
- **Tier 1 Tech Hubs (Bengaluru, NCR/Gurugram, Hyderabad)**: Baseline 100% compensation scale. High living costs, maximum job mobility.
- **Tier 2 Tech Hubs (Pune, Chennai, Mumbai)**: 85%–95% compensation scale; lower housing overhead.
- **Remote Global USD Offers**: 120%–180% local PPP adjusted scale. Requires contractor agreement or Employer of Record (EoR) setup.

## 🚀 Offer Evaluation Checklist
1. **Never evaluate based on CTC alone**: Extract exact Base Cash vs Variable Cash vs Year-1 Stock Vesting.
2. **Calculate Risk-Adjusted Equity Value**: Treat early-stage ESOPs as 0–20% of nominal value unless company has clear Series C+ liquidity history.
3. **Verify Refresher Policies**: Ask recruiters if annual RSU refreshers are standard for your target performance band.
