# Decision Framework: Comparing Multiple Job Offers

## 📌 Executive Summary
Evaluating competing job offers requires analyzing total compensation structure (Base vs Variable vs ESOPs), career trajectory, company financial health, and work-life alignment rather than focusing on CTC title figures alone.

## 📊 Offer Comparison Scoring Matrix
When evaluating Offer A vs Offer B, assign a score (1 to 10) across these 5 core dimensions:

| Dimension | Key Evaluation Criteria | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Cash Payout** | Monthly in-hand base salary after tax & statutory deductions. | 30% |
| **Equity & Upside** | Vested RSUs (public Big Tech) vs ESOPs (early-stage startup risk). | 25% |
| **Role & Scope** | Ownership level, tech stack modernness, promotion velocity. | 20% |
| **Company Stability** | Runway length (>18 months), profitability, market share. | 15% |
| **Work Culture & WLB** | Hybrid flexibility, commute time, team management style. | 10% |

## 💰 Component-Level Financial Trade-Offs
- **Base Salary Payout**: Fixed monthly cash. Essential for rent, EMI servicing, and loan approvals.
- **ESOP Options**: Treat early-stage ESOPs as high-risk upside (discount to 10-20% face value unless company is Series C+).
- **Joining Bonus**: Negotiate one-time joining bonus if replacing forfeited unvested stock or performance bonus.

## 🚀 Counter-Offer Negotiation Strategy
1. **Never fabricate competing offers**: Background checks verify offer letters and start dates.
2. **Leverage Offer B to negotiate Offer A**: Politely communicate *"I have a competing offer at X amount, but your team remains my top choice if we can bridge the base salary gap by Y."*
