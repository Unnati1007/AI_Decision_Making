# Decision Framework: Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) & Promotion Playbook

## 📌 Executive Summary
Navigating performance evaluations requires distinguishing between genuine feedback vs organizational restructuring, and knowing how to build a winning Promotion Document.

## 🚨 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Diagnosis & Strategy

### 1. Genuine PIP vs Quiet Firing Signals
- **Genuine PIP (Fixable)**: Specific, measurable goals with daily/weekly check-ins; clear manager support; company budget is healthy; performance dip was tied to clear personal/health reason.
- **Quiet Firing / Severance Pretext PIP (Exit Immediately)**: Vague/impossible goals; manager unresponsive; sudden top-down headcount reduction target in company; PIP duration < 30 days.

### 2. Strategic Action Matrix on PIP Placement
| Path | Recommended Actions | Risk / Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A: Fight & Pass PIP** | Document daily output in writing; over-communicate progress; request clear metrics approval in email. | High stress; 40-50% pass rate; career growth at company may remain stalled post-PIP. |
| **Option B: Mutual Severance Exit** | Negotiate 2–3 months severance payout in exchange for immediate quiet resignation without PIP record. | Clean resume record; immediate full-time job search focus. |
| **Option C: Parallel Interviewing** | Complete minimal PIP tasks while dedicating 80% energy to DSA/interview prep & external applications. | Recommended if notice period allows smooth transition. |

## 🚀 Promotion Pitch Playbook: Building a Promo Doc
To secure a tier-1 performance rating and promotion:
1. **Maintain a Brag Document**: Record monthly achievements, metric impact, code reviews, and cross-team unblocking.
2. **Align with Next-Level Expectations**: If aiming for Senior SDE (L5), show 6 months of existing performance at L5 level (system design ownership, mentoring).
3. **Quantify Business Value**: Frame impact in terms of `Revenue Generated`, `Cost Reduced`, or `Engineers Unblocked`.
