# Decision Framework: Career Switch & Domain Transition

## 📌 Executive Summary
Switching career domains (e.g., Software Engineer to Product Manager, QA to DevOps, Non-tech to Data Science) requires bridging skill gaps and leveraging transferable skills.

## 🌉 Transition Framework
1. **Identify Transferable Skills**: Analytical thinking, stakeholder management, domain expertise, project execution.
2. **Internal Role Transition**: The highest success rate comes from switching roles within your current company (e.g. moving from dev to APM internally).
3. **Portfolio & Side Projects**: Build tangible proof-of-work projects rather than just collecting online certificates.

## 🎯 Common Transition Paths
- **Engineer ➔ Product Manager**: Highlight user empathy, business metrics, product teardowns, and cross-functional coordination.
- **QA ➔ DevOps / SRE**: Build automation scripts, CI/CD pipeline experience, Docker/Kubernetes containerization hands-on knowledge.
- **Non-Tech ➔ Tech / Analytics**: Master SQL, Python data libraries (Pandas), PowerBI/Tableau, and business domain logic.

## ⏳ Expectations & Trade-offs
- A lateral career switch may temporarily reset seniority level, but long-term trajectory and role satisfaction improve.
