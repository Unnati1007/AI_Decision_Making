# Gig economy

**Source Citation**: [Wikipedia Open Corpus (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gig_economy)
**Domain**: Career

---

The gig economy is the economic system by which a workforce of people engage in freelance or side-employment by corporate entities. This sector of the economy, which is now often performed through digital platforms, has been on the rise in the 21st century.
