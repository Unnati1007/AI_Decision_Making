# Decision Framework: Individual Contributor (IC) vs Managerial Track

## 📌 Executive Summary
As professionals mature, career paths diverge into Individual Contributor (Staff/Principal Engineer/Architect) vs People Manager (Engineering Manager/Director).

## ⚖️ Dual-Track Comparison
| Aspect | Individual Contributor (IC) | People Manager |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Deep technical expertise, architecture, system execution | People growth, team velocity, hiring, strategy |
| **Daily Activity** | Coding, system design, technical reviews | 1:1 meetings, cross-team alignment, budgeting |
| **Success Metric** | System stability, innovation, technical throughput | Team retention, deliverable quality, career progression of reports |

## 🎯 How to Decide
- Choose **IC Track** if you love deep technical problem-solving, building complex systems, and hate administrative/meeting overhead.
- Choose **Manager Track** if you enjoy mentoring people, organizational strategy, team building, and driving business alignment.
