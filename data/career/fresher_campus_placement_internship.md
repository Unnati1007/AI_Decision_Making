# Decision Framework: Fresher Placement Strategy & Internship Selection

## 📌 Executive Summary
For college freshers and early graduates, navigating campus placements, off-campus applications, and PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) conversions sets the baseline trajectory for the first 3 years of career growth.

## 🎯 Campus vs Off-Campus Strategy
- **On-Campus Placements**: High conversion probability due to company-college partnerships. Prioritize securing an initial offer on campus to establish a financial safety net.
- **Off-Campus Applications**: Higher compensation ceiling at top product startups and Big Tech firms. Requires strong GitHub proof-of-work projects, open-source contributions, and LinkedIn networking.

## 💡 Internship to Full-Time PPO Conversion Checklist
1. **Deliver Measurable Impact**: Focus on completing assigned sprint tasks ahead of schedule with clean code documentation.
2. **Seek Weekly Mentor Feedback**: Schedule 15-minute 1-on-1s with your engineering mentor to track performance expectations.
3. **Build Internal Relationships**: Network with team leads and cross-functional engineers to secure strong PPO recommendations.

## 🚀 Service-Based IT Companies vs Product Startups for Freshers
- **Service-Based MNCs (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture)**: Structured training programs, high initial job stability, lower starting compensation (3.5 - 7 LPA).
- **Product Startups & Unicorns**: High learning velocity, modern tech stacks, higher starting compensation (8 - 25 LPA), higher performance expectations.
