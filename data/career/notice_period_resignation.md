# Decision Framework: Resignation Protocol & Notice Period Management

## 📌 Executive Summary
Handling resignations professionally protects your industry reputation, ensures smooth background checks, and manages notice period obligations (typically 30–90 days in India).

## 📋 Standard Resignation Steps
1. **Verbal Notice**: Have a 1:1 conversation with your direct manager before submitting the formal email.
2. **Formal Email**: Submit a concise, polite resignation email to Manager & HR specifying your intended last working date.
3. **Notice Period Buyout**: If your new employer needs an earlier join date, negotiate a notice buyout where the new company pays the shortfall.
4. **Non-Compete Clauses**: Review employment agreements for enforceable vs unenforceable non-compete terms in your jurisdiction.
