# Decision Framework: Salary Negotiation & Compensation Structuring

## 📌 Executive Summary
Salary negotiation is a strategic process backed by market benchmarks, personal value quantification, and timing.

## 💡 Key Strategies
1. **Market Benchmarking**: Research Glassdoor, Level.fyi, and LinkedIn Salary Insights for your specific role, location, and years of experience.
2. **Quantifiable Achievements**: Frame your value proposition around revenue generated, cost savings achieved, or process speedup metrics.
3. **Competing Offers**: Having competing offers provides maximum leverage. Never fabricate an offer, as background checks verify dates.

## 💰 Component Structuring Matrix
- **Fixed Base**: Priority for monthly cash flow and loan eligibility.
- **Variable / Performance Bonus**: Ensure clear KPIs define bonus payout percentages.
- **ESOPs / Equity**: Negotiate vesting schedules (standard 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff).
- **Joining Bonus**: One-time bonus useful to compensate for forfeited bonuses or unvested stock.

## 🚫 Common Negotiation Mistakes
- Accepting the first offer immediately without counter-proposing (most initial offers have 10-15% buffer).
- Negotiating solely on personal expenses rather than market value and business impact.
