# Decision Framework: Professional Certifications vs Degrees vs Projects

## 📌 Executive Summary
Evaluating whether to invest money and time in industry certifications (AWS, PMP, CFA, GCP) vs formal higher education vs hands-on portfolio building.

## 🎯 Value Impact Matrix
- **High ROI Certifications**: Cloud architecture (AWS Solutions Architect, GCP), Financial analysis (CFA), Project Management (PMP) where industry standards mandate credentialing.
- **Low ROI Certifications**: Generic non-proctored online completion certificates that lack practical exam evaluation.
- **Proof-of-Work (Projects)**: For software engineering, data science, and design, deployed applications and GitHub repositories outweigh certificates 3-to-1 in hiring decisions.
