# Decision Framework: Remote vs Hybrid vs On-Site Work Decisions

## 📌 Executive Summary
Choosing between fully remote, hybrid (2-3 days office), and full on-site roles involves balancing commute time, salary, team visibility, and personal productivity.

## ⚖️ Trade-off Evaluation
- **Fully Remote**: Saves 1.5–3 hours daily commute time, geographical flexibility, but requires strict self-discipline and proactive communication to stay visible for promotions.
- **Hybrid**: Blends collaborative face-to-face team alignment with deep focus work days at home.
- **On-Site**: Higher serendipitous networking, immediate mentorship, better for early-career professionals (0-3 years experience).
