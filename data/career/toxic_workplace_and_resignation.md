# Exiting a Toxic Workplace & Strategic Resignation

Navigating a toxic workplace environment (unreasonable workload, psychological strain, poor leadership, lack of psychological safety) requires a calm, risk-managed exit strategy.

## 1. Safety Buffer & Financial Assessment
Before resigning without another job offer in hand:
- **Emergency Runway**: Ensure you have $\ge 6 \text{ months}$ of liquid living expenses in your emergency fund.
- **Health & Mental Buffer**: If physical or severe psychological distress is present, consult medical guidance first and check medical leave options before immediate quit.

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Step 1: Quiet Boundaries & Documentation
- Strictly adhere to contract hours without over-extending.
- Document all work outputs, emails, and performance feedback externally for reference.

### Step 2: Confidential Job Hunt
- Update resume and LinkedIn settings to "Open to Work (Recruiters Only)".
- Schedule interviews around official work breaks or approved time-off.

### Step 3: Professional Resignation
- Keep the resignation letter concise and objective (state last working date without emotional vent).
- Fulfill notice period obligations professionally to preserve background verification credentials.
