# Decision Framework: Freelancing / Contracting vs Full-Time Job

## 📌 Executive Summary
Transitioning from full-time employment to independent contracting or freelancing balances autonomy and income ceiling against income stability and benefits.

## ⚖️ Comparative Framework
- **Full-Time Job**: Predictable paycheck, health insurance, paid time off, structured career progression, but income is capped by salary grade.
- **Freelancing / Contracting**: Higher hourly rates, tax write-offs for business expenses, time flexibility, but requires self-funding healthcare, retirement, and continuous client acquisition.

## 📊 Financial Threshold Rule
- Only transition to full-time freelancing when your side-freelance income reaches **75% of your full-time monthly salary** consistently for 6 consecutive months.
