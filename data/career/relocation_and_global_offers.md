# Global Job Relocation & International Offer Evaluation

Evaluating an international job offer or relocation proposal requires analyzing factors far beyond net nominal salary.

## 1. Key Evaluation Pillars

### A. Cost of Living & Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)
- **Gross Salary vs Real Disposable Income**: A \$120,000 offer in San Francisco or London may yield less net savings than a \$70,000 offer in Berlin, Toronto, or Bangalore due to housing costs, taxation, and healthcare.
- **Housing Costs**: Ensure rent consumes $\le 30-35\%$ of net take-home pay.

### B. Visa, Immigration & Work Permit Risk
- **Sponsorship Type**: H-1B, L-1, Blue Card, PR vs dependent visa rules.
- **Tenure Requirements**: Time required to achieve permanent residency or job mobility without employer sponsorship.
- **Spouse Work Rights**: Verify if dependent visas allow immediate employment.

### C. Taxation & Social Security
- **Double Taxation Treaties (DTAA)**: Tax obligations in host vs home country.
- **Mandatory Pension / Healthcare**: Deductions for compulsory health insurance and pension plans.

## 2. Decision Tree & Checklist
1. **Financial Multiplier**: Is Net Savings (Host City) $\ge 1.3 \times$ Net Savings (Home City)?
2. **Career Acceleration**: Does the role provide international cross-cultural team management or high-growth tech exposure?
3. **Exit / Safety Buffer**: Does the package include relocation allowance, temporary housing (30-60 days), and return flight coverage in case of early contract termination?
