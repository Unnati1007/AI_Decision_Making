# Decision Framework: Startup vs Corporate Career Paths

## 📌 Executive Summary
Choosing between an early/growth-stage startup and an established corporate MNC determines your learning velocity, job security, compensation structure, and work culture.

## 🏢 Comparison Breakdown
| Metric | Startup (Seed / Series A-B) | Corporate MNC / Big Tech |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Learning Velocity** | High (Multi-hat ownership, fast iterations) | Specialized (Deep domain focus, established processes) |
| **Job Security** | Moderate to Low (Runway dependent) | High (Established revenue streams) |
| **Compensation** | Lower Base + ESOPs/Equity Upside | Higher Base + Bonuses + Predictable Perks |
| **Work-Life Balance** | High pressure, fast paced | Structured hours, clear process bounds |

## 🎯 Stage-Wise Startup Decision Matrix
- **Early Stage (Seed/Series A)**: Join if you want maximum ownership, rapid promotion, and are willing to take financial risk for equity upside.
- **Growth Stage (Series B/C)**: Best balance of scale, learning, decent compensation, and reduced failure risk.
- **Corporate / MNC**: Best for financial stability, structured mentorship, global mobility, and brand prestige.

## ⚠️ Risk & Mitigation Strategies
- **Startup Failure Risk**: Evaluate startup runway (minimum 18 months preferred), founder pedigree, and product-market fit before joining.
- **Corporate Stagnation Risk**: Set 18-month goals for internal promotions or project rotations to prevent skill obsolescence.
