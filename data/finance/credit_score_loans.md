# Decision Framework: Credit Score Building & Credit Card Optimization

## 📌 Executive Summary
Maintaining a strong CIBIL/Credit Score (750+) to secure lower interest rates on future home and vehicle loans.

## 📋 Credit Building Rules
1. **Credit Utilization Ratio**: Keep total monthly credit card spend **under 30% of total credit limit**.
2. **On-Time Payment History**: Set up auto-debit for 100% of statement balance (never pay minimum amount due).
3. **Credit Age & Inquiries**: Avoid applying for multiple credit cards or personal loans within short time spans (hard inquiries).
