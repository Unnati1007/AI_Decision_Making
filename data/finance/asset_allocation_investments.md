# Decision Framework: Asset Allocation & Investment Strategy

## 📌 Executive Summary
Effective wealth creation requires balancing risk and return through systematic asset allocation rather than speculative market timing.

## 📊 Standard Portfolio Allocation Rule
- **Equity (60%)**: Low-cost Index Funds (Nifty 50, S&P 500) + Flexi-cap mutual funds for long-term compounding (7+ years).
- **Debt (20%)**: PPF, Debt Mutual Funds, Sovereign Gold Bonds for capital preservation and rebalancing liquidity.
- **Direct Stocks / Alpha (20%)**: Direct equity research for high-growth potential.

## 💡 Key Investment Principles
1. **Rule of 100 Minus Age**: Percentage allocated to equity = 100 - your age (e.g., at age 25, 75% in equity).
2. **SIP Disciplinary Compounding**: Consistent monthly SIP investments perform better over 10-year horizons than trying to time market bottoms.
