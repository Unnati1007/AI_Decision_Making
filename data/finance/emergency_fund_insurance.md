# Decision Framework: Emergency Funds & Insurance Coverage

## 📌 Executive Summary
Financial security requires an emergency cash cushion and risk transfer via health and term insurance before aggressive investing.

## 🛡️ The Financial Safety Net Hierarchy
1. **Emergency Fund**: 6 months of mandatory living expenses kept in liquid funds or high-yield savings accounts.
2. **Term Insurance**: Pure risk protection cover equal to **10x to 15x annual income**. Avoid investment-linked insurance plans (ULIPs/endowment plans).
3. **Health Insurance**: Base policy of ₹10L–₹25L for self & immediate family + super top-up policy.
