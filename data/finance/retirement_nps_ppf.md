# Decision Framework: Retirement Planning — NPS vs PPF vs EPF

## 📌 Executive Summary
Building a inflation-protected retirement corpus using government-backed pension and provident fund schemes.

## ⚖️ Scheme Comparison
- **EPF (Employees Provident Fund)**: Mandatory 12% basic contribution, high interest (8%+), tax-free at maturity.
- **PPF (Public Provident Fund)**: 15-year lock-in, tax-free interest and maturity (EEE status), capped at ₹1.5L/year.
- **NPS (National Pension System)**: Market-linked retirement account with equity allocation (up to 75%), extra ₹50k tax benefit under 80CCD(1B).
