# Decision Framework: Alternative Assets & Crypto Exposure Limits

## 📌 Executive Summary
Allocating capital to alternative asset classes like Gold, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), REITS, and Cryptocurrency.

## 📊 Allocation Ceiling Rules
- **Gold / SGB**: 5-10% of total portfolio as a hedge against inflation and currency depreciation.
- **REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)**: Provides real estate rental yield exposure with low capital requirements and daily liquidity.
- **Crypto / High Risk Speculative Assets**: Capped strictly at **< 5% of investable surplus** due to extreme volatility and regulatory taxation (30% tax + 1% TDS in India).
