# Decision Framework: Direct Equity vs Index Investing

## 📌 Executive Summary
Evaluating direct stock selection vs passive index fund investing for individual investors.

## 📊 Comparison Strategy
- **Direct Equity**: Requires fundamental balance sheet analysis, time for quarterly tracking, and high risk appetite.
- **Passive Indexing**: 85% of active fund managers fail to beat benchmark indices over a 10-year period. Index funds provide market-matching returns with minimal management costs.
