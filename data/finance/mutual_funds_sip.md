# Decision Framework: Mutual Funds & Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Choosing the right category of mutual funds based on investment horizon and risk tolerance.

## 📊 Mutual Fund Category Guide
- **Large Cap / Nifty 50 Index Funds**: Low expense ratio (< 0.2%), core foundation of long-term portfolios, low volatility.
- **Flexi-Cap / Mid-Cap Funds**: Higher growth potential over 5-7 year horizons.
- **Small-Cap Funds**: High volatility, limit allocation to < 15% of total portfolio.
- **Liquid / Overnight Funds**: For short-term capital deployment (< 1 year).
