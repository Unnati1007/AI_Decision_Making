# Decision Framework: Debt Payoff vs Market Investing

## 📌 Executive Summary
Deciding whether to prepay existing loans or invest extra cash in equity markets.

## ⚖️ Interest Rate Benchmark Rule
- **High Interest Debt (> 10% p.a.)**: Credit cards (36-42%), Personal loans (12-18%). **Prepay immediately** before investing.
- **Low Interest Debt (< 8.5% p.a.)**: Home loans, education loans. Pay standard EMI and invest extra cash in equities where historical CAGR exceeds 12%.
