# Decision Framework: Tax Saving & Regime Optimization (India)

## 📌 Executive Summary
Maximizing post-tax income requires choosing between the Old Tax Regime and New Tax Regime and utilizing valid statutory deductions.

## 💰 Major Deductions (Old Regime)
- **Section 80C (up to ₹1,500,000)**: ELSS Tax Saving Mutual Funds (3-year lock-in), PPF, EPF, Senior Citizen Savings Scheme.
- **Section 80CCD(1B) NPS (up to ₹50,000)**: Additional deduction for National Pension System.
- **Section 80D Health Insurance**: Up to ₹25,000 for self/family + ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 for parents.
- **HRA Exemption**: Minimum of (Actual HRA received, Rent paid - 10% of basic, 50% basic for metro).

## ⚖️ Old vs New Tax Regime Rule of Thumb
- If your total eligible deductions exceed **₹3.75 Lakhs to ₹4.25 Lakhs** annually, Old Tax Regime usually yields lower tax liability. Otherwise, New Tax Regime is simpler and cheaper.
